About

Behind the glam

Makeup artist

Roshni Ravi

I'm a bridal makeup artist based in Chennai. My journey into makeup started from Visual Communication and a career in media, working as an Assistant Director at Star Vijay and later in advertising. Makeup had quietly been my passion for years, so I decided to pursue it professionally and trained at Chic Studios in New York.

After returning to Chennai, I worked on fashion and brand shoots before finding my way into bridal makeup. For the past 10 years, I've been part of brides' most special moments. I love the intimacy of wedding mornings, the emotions, the excitement, and the trust that comes with what I do.

My style is soft, elegant, and skin-focused. I believe makeup should enhance you, not change you, so you still look like yourself, just more confident and radiant.

Hair stylist

Sainidhi

With over 10 years of experience in the bridal industry, Sainidhi brings skill, creativity, and calm to every bride she works with. Professionally trained in cut, colour, and hairstyling at Bounce Academy, she built a strong technical foundation before discovering her calling in bridal hairstyling.

Her interest in hair science and structure lets her design styles that are elegant, long-lasting, and tailored to each bride's hair type, comfort, and personality. Known for her gentle presence and attention to detail, she creates looks that feel effortless yet refined.

Together

Working together

We began our bridal journey together with our very first bride and have been partners ever since. Our brides often talk about the energy we bring to the wedding morning: fun, easy, and calming. We make sure every bride feels relaxed, confident, and taken care of.
